# Shared maturity model

Every chapter's `## Maturity model` section uses this same five-level scale, applied to that chapter's specific topic. Do not rename the levels or change their order.

| Level | Name | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Initiate** | The organization recognizes the issue but has no consistent practice. Activity is ad hoc, dependent on individuals, and undocumented. |
| 2 | **Develop** | Practices exist but are inconsistent across teams or services. Early tools and templates exist but are not mandated. |
| 3 | **Standardize** | A common approach is documented and adopted organization-wide. Roles and accountabilities are defined. |
| 4 | **Manage** | Performance against the standard is measured and reported. Gaps trigger corrective action. Practice is resourced, not just mandated. |
| 5 | **Orchestrate** | The organization coordinates the practice across its boundary — with other public bodies, delivery partners, and citizens — and adapts it proactively as conditions change. |

A chapter's maturity table has 3–5 rows, each row a sub-dimension of the chapter's topic (for example, Chapter 2.1's rows might be "outcomes are defined," "outcomes are measured," "the scorecard informs resourcing decisions"), with a short phrase per level per row — not a repeat of this page's generic descriptions.
