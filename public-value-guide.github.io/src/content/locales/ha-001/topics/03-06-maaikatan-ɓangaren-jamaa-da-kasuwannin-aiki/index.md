# Batu 3.6 — Ma'aikatan ɓangaren jama'a da kasuwannin aiki

**Dabarar gwamnati ba ta fi kyau fiye da alaƙar aiki da ke aiwatar da ita ba, kuma kasuwar aiki da ta bambanta da ta masu zaman kansu ce ke tsara waɗannan alaƙa ta hanyoyin da kowane shugaban jama'a yake buƙatar ambato, ba a ɗauka cewa ba su wanzu ba.**

## Dalilin da ya sa wannan yake da muhimmanci ga darajar jama'a

Kowace shawarar daraja ta jama'a, komai kyawun hujjarta, wani ne yake isar da ita: ma'aikacin shari'a, injiniya, ma'aikaciyar jinya, jami'in tsara birane, ƙwararren sayayya. Ƙafar ƙarfin aiki ta kusurwar dabara (duba Batu 1.2 — Kusurwar dabara mai uku: halacci, daraja da iyawa) a aikace galibi tambayar ma'aikata ce — ko ƙungiyar tana da mutanen da suka dace, a yawan da ya dace, masu ƙwarewar da ta dace, waɗanda suke son zama tsawon lokaci don gina ilimin cibiya da babbar manufa mai rikitarwa take buƙata. Ma'aikatar kuɗi za ta iya amincewa da kowane layin kasafin kuɗi da hidima take buƙata kuma har yanzu ta ga isarwa ta gaza saboda injiniyoyin ƙwararru ko masu nazarin bayanai da take buƙata ba za su yi aiki ba ga abin da doka ta ba ta damar biya.

Wannan ba matsalar albarkatun ɗan adam ce ta gama-gari da aka shigo da ita cikin gwamnati ba; kasuwar aikin ɓangaren jama'a tana da siffofin tsari da mai aiki mai zaman kansa da wuya ya fuskanta. Ana kafa albashi, a wani sashi aƙalla, ta hanyar tsarin siyasa da yake bayyane ga masu zaɓe da 'yan siyasar adawa ta hanyar da bitar albashi ta masu zaman kansu ba ta taɓa kasancewa ba. A sana'o'i da ƙwararru da yawa, mai aiki ɗaya — ƙasa — shi ne babban mai saya ko ɗaya tilo na wani nau'in aiki a wani yanki, wanda yake canza tushen tattalin arzikin dangantakar. Kuma babban kaso na ma'aikatan ɓangaren jama'a da na zamantakewa suna bayar da rahoton cewa wani abu fiye da albashi yana motsa su, tsarin da aka rubuta wanda shugabannin jama'a za su iya amfani da shi da alhaki ko su yi amfani da shi a ɓoye.

Yin kuskure a tambayar ma'aikata yana kashe fiye da kuɗi. Ma'aikatan gwamnati da ke ɗaukar ma'aikata da ɗaga su bisa goyon baya na sirri maimakon cancanta suna rasa halaccin da ke zuwa daga ganin suna aiki ba tare da son zuciya ba. Tsarin albashi da yake watsi da gaskiyar kasuwa yana zubar da ƙwarewar ƙwararru da hukuma ta zamani ba za ta iya aiki ba tare da su ba. Shirin ma'aikata da yake watsi da sauyin alƙaluman jama'a yana gano giɓin iyawarsa ne kawai lokacin da dukan zamani na ƙwaƙwalwar cibiya ya yi ritaya cikin 'yan shekaru kaɗan da juna. Wannan batu yana bayyana ra'ayoyi da zaɓuɓɓukan da suke ƙayyade ko mutanen ƙungiya dukiya ce da ta gina da gangan ko haɗari da ta gada ba tare da niyya ba.

## Muhimman ra'ayoyi

**Ma'aikatan gwamnati (civil service)** gungun jami'ai ne na dindindin, ƙwararru, waɗanda suke ma'aikata a gudanar da gwamnati kuma suna ci gaba da aiki yayin da shugabannin da aka zaɓa suke canzawa (duba [Ma'aikatan gwamnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_service)). Wannan batu yana ɗaukar ma'aikatan gwamnati a matsayin ma'aikata — yadda ake ɗaukar su, biyan su, tsara su, da shirya musu — yana ginawa a kan yadda aka bi da ma'aikatan gwamnati a Batu 3.1 — Tsare-tsare da samfuran gudanar da mulkin jama'a, a matsayin tsarin cibiya cikin faɗin nau'ukan tsarin tunani na gudanar da mulkin jama'a (duba Batu 3.1 — Tsare-tsare da samfuran gudanar da mulkin jama'a, wanda shi ne babban batun samfurin tsarin ofisoshi na Max Weber da magadansa).

Ɗaukar ma'aikatan gwamnati da tsarin sana'o'i suna faɗawa, a faɗaɗe, cikin iyalai biyu, kuma yawancin ainihin tsare-tsare haɗakar biyun ne. **Tsarin sana'a (career-based system)** yana ɗaukar ma'aikata galibi a matakin shiga, yawanci kai tsaye daga ilimi, kuma yana tsammanin yawancin ma'aikata su yi rayuwar aiki a cikin hukumar, suna ɗagawa ta hanyar ƙayyadadden matsayi; *grands corps* na Faransa, ma'aikatan gwamnatin ƙasa ta Japan, da hanyar *fast stream* ta gargajiya ta Birtaniya ana ambaton su a matsayin misalai. **Tsarin mukami (position-based system)** yana ɗaukar ma'aikata dabam ga kowane mukami da ke wofi, a kowane mataki, daga cikin gwamnati ko wajenta, yana tantance ƴan takara bisa takamaiman aikin maimakon hanyar sana'a ta gama-gari; gwamnatin tarayya ta Amurka da wasu masu gyara na Nordic da na masu jin Turanci na shekarun 1980 da 1990 sun karkata ƙari zuwa wannan samfurin. Tsarin sana'a yana gina amincewar cibiya mai zurfi amma yana iya jinkirin kawo ƙwarewar waje mai ƙaranci; tsarin mukami yana ɗaukar ƙwararrun ma'aikata cikin sauƙi amma yana iya raunana ainihin haɗin kai kuma ya sa tsara maye gurbi ya zama mai wuya. Ƙungiyar Haɗin Gwiwar Tattalin Arziki da Ci Gaba (OECD) tana bin diddigin wannan bambanci a ƙasashen mambobinta, kuma yawancin tsare-tsare a yau suna haɗa matsayin gama-gari na hanyar sana'a da ɗaukar ƙwararru bisa mukami don ƙwarewa kamar isarwa ta dijital ko nazarin kididdigar inshora.

Duka samfuran yawanci an gina su ne a kan **tsarin cancanta (merit system)**: ƙa'idar cewa ɗaukar ma'aikata da ɗaga su a aikin gwamnati ya kamata a yanke shi bisa iya aiki, da aka tantance ta gasa mai buɗewa da adalci, maimakon alaƙar siyasa (duba [Tsarin cancanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merit_system)). An gina tsarin cancanta, a tarihi, a martani ga **tsarin ganima (spoils system)**, inda sabuwar jam'iyyar da aka zaɓa take cika mukaman gwamnati da magoya bayanta ba tare da la'akari da ƙwarewa ba (duba [Tsarin ganima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spoils_system)). Amurka ta tashi daga cikakken goyon baya na sirri tare da Dokar Gyaran Ma'aikatan Gwamnati ta Pendleton ta 1883, wadda ta kafa jarrabawa ta gasa don mukaman tarayya da yawa (duba [Dokar Gyaran Ma'aikatan Gwamnati ta Pendleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pendleton_Civil_Service_Reform_Act)); abin da ya yi daidai da shi a Birtaniya shi ne Rahoton Northcote–Trevelyan na 1854, wanda ya ba da shawarar ɗaukar ma'aikata ta jarrabawa a buɗe da ɗaga su bisa nasara maimakon goyon baya na sirri (duba [Rahoton Northcote–Trevelyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northcote%E2%80%93Trevelyan_Report)). Gyare-gyaren biyu har yanzu suna da muhimmanci domin tashin hankalin da suka magance — ikon siyasa kan naɗi da hidimar ƙwararru mara ɓangaranci — yana sake bayyana a duk lokacin da sabuwar gwamnati da aka zaɓa take son mutanenta a mukami da sauri.

Ana kafa albashin ɓangaren jama'a ta hanyar haɗin hukumomin kafa albashi na gudanarwa, **yarjejeniyar ƙungiya ta gamayya (collective bargaining)** ta ƙasa ko ta fanni — tattaunawa tsakanin mai aiki da ƙungiyoyin ma'aikata da aka tsara, yawanci ƙungiyoyin ƙwadago, kan albashi da sharuɗɗa (duba [Yarjejeniyar ƙungiya ta gamayya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collective_bargaining)) — kuma, a ƙasashe da yawa, tsarin bitar albashi mai zaman kansa da aka tsara don riƙe shawarwarin albashi masu mahimmanci nesa da ministoci. **Ƙungiyar ƙwadago (trade union)**, ƙungiyar ma'aikata da aka kafa don kare muradunsu na gamayya na albashi, sharuɗɗa, da tsaron aiki, sau da yawa mai tasiri ce kuma mafi kafu a cibiyance a ɓangaren jama'a fiye da a yawancin masana'antu masu zaman kansu, a wani ɓangare domin yawan shiga ƙungiyar ƙwadago a ɓangaren jama'a ya fi jurewa fiye da na masu zaman kansu a yawancin ƙasashe masu ci gaba (duba [Ƙungiyar ƙwadago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_union)). Ƙungiyar Ƙwadago ta Ƙasa da Ƙasa (ILO), hukumar Majalisar Ɗinkin Duniya da take kafa kuma take sa ido kan ƙa'idodin ƙwadago na ƙasa da ƙasa, tana magance wannan a cikin Yarjejeniyarta ta Dangantakar Ƙwadago (Aikin Gwamnati), 1978 (Yarjejeniya mai lamba 151), wadda take kira ga ƙasashe su kare haƙƙin ma'aikatan gwamnati na tsara kansu da samar da tsare-tsare masu adalci don ƙayyade sharuɗɗan aikinsu (duba [Ƙungiyar Ƙwadago ta Ƙasa da Ƙasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Labour_Organization) da [Yarjejeniyar Dangantakar Ƙwadago (Aikin Gwamnati), 1978](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Relations_(Public_Service)_Convention,_1978)).

Matsayin gwamnati a matsayin mai aiki kuma yana da tsarin kasuwa na musamman. **Mai saya ɗaya (monopsony)** kasuwa ce da mai saya ɗaya yake ɗaukar mafi yawa ko duka buƙatar wani kaya ko hidima, yana ba wa wannan mai saya ikon tasiri kan farashi zuwa ƙasa da abin da kasuwar gasa za ta kafa (duba [Mai saya ɗaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopsony)). Gwamnatin ƙaramar hukuma da ta ƙasa sau da yawa ita ce mai aiki mai saya ɗaya ko kusan mai saya ɗaya ga sana'o'i kamar koyarwa, jinya, kashe gobara, da aikin zamantakewa a wani yanki, wanda yake ba wa kafa albashi na ɓangaren jama'a ƙarin ikon rage albashi ƙasa da matakin gasarsa fiye da yawancin masu aiki masu zaman kansu, amma kuma ƙarin alhakin sakamakon, domin ba za a iya samun wani wuri a gida ga ƙwararren ma'aikaci a wannan fanni ya tafi ba. A kan haka akwai ganuwar siyasa: ba da albashin ɓangaren jama'a, ba kamar shawarar albashin masu zaman kansu ba, yawanci ana muhawara a kansa a majalisa kuma gwamnati da adawa duka suna ɗaukarsa a matsayin sigina game da horon kuɗi, wanda ya sa shawarwarin albashi su yi jinkiri kuma su fi ƙuntatawa ta abubuwan da ba na kasuwar aiki ba fiye da aikin masu zaman kansu makamancinsa (duba Batu 3.3 — Kuɗin jama'a da tsara kasafin kuɗi don yadda kuɗin albashi yake a cikin faɗin tsarin kasafin kuɗi).

**Ƙwarin gwiwar hidimar jama'a (public service motivation, PSM)** shi ne halin da aka rubuta na mutane da yawa da suke aiki a gwamnati da ƙungiyoyi masu zaman kansu na ƙwarin gwiwa, a wani sashi aƙalla, ta sha'awar yi wa muradin jama'a hidima, maimakon ƙarfafawa ta albashi da sana'a kaɗai (duba [Ƙwarin gwiwar hidimar jama'a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_service_motivation)). Masanan gudanar da mulkin jama'a na Amurka James L. Perry da Lois Recascino Wise ne suka ƙirƙiri kalmar a labarin 1990 da ya bayyana dalilan hidimar jama'a na hankali, na ƙa'ida, da na ji, kuma tun daga lokacin ya zama ɗaya daga cikin ra'ayoyin da aka fi gwadawa a binciken gudanar da mulkin jama'a. PSM tana da muhimmanci a nan domin tana canza ma'anar "samun ƙarfafawa daidai": mai aiki da ke zaton ma'aikata suna amsa lada na kuɗi kawai zai yi kuskuren tsara ɗaukar ma'aikata (yana rasa ƴan takarar da manufa ta jawo hankalinsu) da tsarin lada (yana gabatar da albashin aiki da binciken kan "korar" ƙwarin gwiwa ke nuna zai iya, a wasu wurare, raunana ƙoƙarin da ke fitowa daga ciki maimakon ƙarfafa shi).

**Shirin ma'aikata (workforce planning)** shi ne horon hasashen buƙatun ma'aikata na gaba na ƙungiya — bisa adadi, wuri, da ƙwarewa — a kan yiwuwar samar da mutane a gaba, da rufe giɓin da gangan maimakon a mayar da martani. A ɓangaren jama'a wannan dole ne ya yi la'akari da tsufan alƙaluman jama'a da ya taru a wasu sana'o'i (injiniya, kiwon lafiya, da ayyukan bincike da aka ɗauka a guguwar faɗaɗawa ta bayan yaƙi misali ne da ke maimaitawa a ƙasashen OECD), sauye-sauyen ƙwarewa da isarwa ta dijital ke tuƙawa, da dakatarwar albashi ko ɗaukar ma'aikata da aka sanya saboda dalilan kuɗi da za su iya bar giɓi ba a cika ba tsawon shekaru. Ɓangaren zamantakewa yana ƙara wani matakin da ma'aikatan gwamnati da wuya su sarrafa: ƙungiyoyi marasa riba da na al'umma sau da yawa suna gudanar da **ma'aikata haɗaka (blended workforce)** na ma'aikata masu albashi da masu sa kai — mutanen da suke ba da lokaci da ƙoƙari da yardar rai ba tare da tsammanin biya ba (duba [Sa kai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volunteering)) — ana tsara su tare, wanda yake haɗa shirin ma'aikata da bi da 'yan ƙasa da masu sa kai a matsayin masu haɗin gwiwar samar da darajar jama'a, ba kawai masu sauraron hidima ba (duba Batu 1.5 — Masu ruwa da tsaki, 'yan ƙasa da haɗin gwiwar samarwa).

## Ingantattun ayyuka

1. **Ku yanke shawara, da gangan, ko aiki yana cikin hanyar sana'a ko ta mukami.** Ku bayyana a fili waɗanne ayyuka suke buƙatar dorewar cibiya mai zurfi da waɗanne suke buƙatar ƙwarewa ta musamman da bututun cikin gida ba zai samar da tabbaci ba.
2. **Ku buga ƙa'idar ɗaukar ma'aikata da kuke bi a zahiri, ba wadda kuke zaton kowa yana bi ba.** Cancanta, adalci, da gasa a buɗe ba su da daraja idan masu ɗaukar ma'aikata, kwamitoci, da waɗanda aka naɗa a siyasa ba duka suna aiki daga tsarin ƙa'idodi da aka buga ɗaya ba.
3. **Ku ɗauki tsarin bitar albashi a matsayin tushen halacci, ba cikas da za a sarrafa ba.** Tsarin kafa albashi mai zaman kansa, wanda aka ba shi kayan aiki yadda ya kamata, yana kare ma'aikata daga dakatarwa ta son rai kuma yana kare ministoci daga zargin sayen ƙuri'u da kuɗin albashi.
4. **Ku yi yarjejeniyar ƙungiya ta gamayya a zagayen shekaru da yawa duk inda tsarin kuɗi ya ba da dama.** Zagayen shekara ɗaya, na gaba-da-gaba suna cinye hankalin gudanarwa kuma suna ruguza amana; tsarin da aka sake duba maimakon a sake yin ƙara kowace shekara yana ba wa ɓangarorin biyu tabbacin tsarawa.
5. **Ku san inda ƙungiyarku take mai aiki mai saya ɗaya, kuma ku saka wannan alhaki cikin farashin shawarwarin albashi.** Idan ba da albashinku a zahiri shi ne kasuwar aikin wata sana'a a yankinku, rashin biya ba kawai yana ceton kuɗi ba ne; yana iya rage dukan samar da wannan ƙwarewa a gida.
6. **Ku tsara tsarin lada a kan abin da yake motsa ma'aikatanku a zahiri, ba zato na gama-gari game da ƙarfafawa ba.** Inda ƙwarin gwiwar hidimar jama'a yake da ƙarfi, ku zuba jari a bayyanar manufa da 'yancin kai kafin ku kai ga kyautar aiki, wadda binciken kan "korar" ƙwarin gwiwa yake nuna wani lokaci zai iya raunana ƙoƙarin da kuke niyyar saka wa.
7. **Ku gudanar da shirin ma'aikata mai birgima, ba aikin ƙidayar kai na lokaci ɗaya ba.** Ku bi diddigin tsarin shekaru, haɗin ƙwarewa, da tsarin ficewar sana'o'inku masu muhimmanci aƙalla kowane shekara biyu zuwa uku, kuma ku ɗauki guguwar ritaya da ke zuwa a matsayin haɗarin kuɗi, ba abin mamaki ba.
8. **Ku ba da kuɗi da ma'aikata ga haɗin kan masu sa kai da muhimmanci kamar ɗaukar ma'aikata masu albashi, duk inda ma'aikata haɗaka suke cikin samfurin.** Gudanar da masu sa kai — tantancewa, horo, tsara jadawali, yabo — aiki ne na ƙwararru na gaske; ɗaukarsa a matsayin abin da aka manta shi ne dalilin da ya fi yawa da shirye-shiryen da suke dogara da masu sa kai ba sa isar da isasshe.
9. **Ku kare tsaka-tsakin siyasa na naɗin ma'aikatan gwamnati a matsayin horo daban da shirin ma'aikata.** Ku riƙe tsarin cika mukamai na dindindin a bayyane a rabe da naɗin masu ba da shawara na siyasa, domin canjin gwamnati ya kada ya jawo komawa ga tsarin ganima ba bisa ƙa'ida ba.
10. **Ku bincika daidaiton albashi bisa aiki, ba matsakaitan kanun labarai kaɗai ba.** Jimillar alƙaluma na iya ɓoye rashin biyan wasu sana'o'i ko rukunai na tsari da matsakaicin ƙungiya gaba ɗaya zai rufe.
11. **Ku ba manajoji ainihin ikon da aka ba su kayan aiki kan ɗaukar ma'aikata da riƙe su, ba kawai maƙasudin yawan ma'aikata ba.** Shirin ma'aikata da yake kafa lambobi a tsakiya amma yana hana manajojin gaba kasafin ɗaukar ma'aikata ko haƙƙin yanke shawara za a kewaye shi maimakon a bi shi.
12. **Ku gaya wa ma'aikata, da harshe mai sauƙi, abin da ƙungiya za ta iya kuma ba za ta iya alƙawari ba game da albashi, tsaro, da ci gaba.** Rashin bayyana yana ruguza amana da sauri fiye da gaskiya, bayanin ƙuntatawa mara daɗi da wuri.

## Tambayoyi don tattaunawa da ƙungiya

**Shin da gaske mun san ko tsarin ɗaukar ma'aikatanmu na sana'a ne, na mukami ne, ko haɗakar biyun da ba a yanke shawara ba?** Ƙungiyoyi da yawa suna gadar tsarin haɗaka ba tare da wani ya zaɓe shi da gangan ba, wanda yake bayyana a ƙa'idodi marasa daidaito na shiga ta gefe, tsammanin ɗagawa marar bayyana, da gogayya tsakanin ma'aikata masu tsawon hidima da ƙwararrun da aka ɗauka daga waje. Ku bi manyan naɗinku goma na ƙarshe ku rarraba kowanne a matsayin an ɗaga shi a cikin gida ko an ɗauko shi daga waje. Ku tattauna ko tsarin da kuke gani ya dace da tsarin da kuke so a zahiri, kuma idan ba haka ba, menene zai buƙaci canzawa a manufar ɗaukar ma'aikatanku don rufe giɓin.

**A ina muke mai aiki mai saya ɗaya ga wata sana'a, kuma muna saka farashin wannan alhaki daidai?** Ku gano ayyukan ƙungiyarku da kuke babban mai aiki ko ɗaya tilo na gida na wannan ƙwarewa, sannan ku kwatanta tayin albashinku da mafi kyawun ma'aunin yanki da ake da shi. Ku tattauna abin da kuka sani game da yawan mukamai maras kowa da lokacin cike su ga waɗannan ayyuka, domin giɓi mai dawwama babban sigina ne cewa albashinku ya faɗi ƙasa da matakin da ake buƙata don ɗorewar samar da wannan ƙwarewa a gida. Ku yi la'akari da abin da zai kashe don rufe giɓin a kan abin da yake kashe ku a yanzu a mukamai maras kowa, aikin ƙari, da maye gurbin hukumar ɗaukar ma'aikata.

**Shin muna dogara da ƙwarin gwiwar hidimar jama'a a matsayin maye gurbin isasshen albashi da sharuɗɗa?** Ƙwarin gwiwar hidimar jama'a na gaske ne kuma mai daraja, amma ba shi da iyaka, kuma ƙungiyar da take ɗaukar jajircewar ma'aikata a hankali a matsayin albarkatun kyauta da za a ci gaba da ɗauka daga gare ta har abada a ƙarshe za ta cinye shi. Ku tattauna da gaskiya ko samfurin ma'aikatanku na yanzu zai tsira idan ma'aikatanku masu mafi ƙarfin manufa suka yanke shawarar an ɗauki jajircewarsu a banza sau ɗaya fiye da kima. Ku gano canji ɗaya na zahiri — a nauyin aiki, yabo, ko 'yancin kai — da zai nuna ƙungiya tana darajanta wannan ƙwarin gwiwa maimakon ta cuci shi.

**Yaya ma'aikatanmu za su kasance cikin shekaru goma idan babu abin da ya canza game da yadda muke ɗaukar ma'aikata a yau?** Ku yi hasashen tsarin shekarun ayyukanku masu muhimmanci na fasaha da ƙwararru gaba shekaru goma ta amfani da tsarin ritaya na yanzu, kuma ku zama takamaimai game da waɗanne ayyuka ne suke fuskantar mafi tsananin dutsen iyawa. Ku tattauna ko bututun ɗaukar ma'aikatanku na yanzu, shirin koyon sana'a ko na masu kammala karatu, da aikin canja wurin ilimi sun dace da maye gurbin abin da za a rasa, ko kuna shirin gano giɓin ne kawai lokacin da ya buɗe. Inda amsar ba ta da daɗi, ku yanke shawara abin da za ku iya fara yi a zahiri yanzu maimakon zagayen kasafin kuɗi na gaba.

**Idan muna dogara da masu sa kai tare da ma'aikata masu albashi, muna ba da kayan aiki ga wannan dangantaka da muhimmanci kamar ma'aikata masu albashi?** Ku lissafa kowane shiri a ƙungiyarku da ba zai yi aiki ba sai da ƙoƙarin masu sa kai, kuma ku duba ko kowanne yana da mai gudanarwa da aka ba kuɗi, aka horar, mai alhakinsa, ko ana ɗaukar gudanar da masu sa kai ba bisa ƙa'ida ba cikin nauyin aikin ma'aikata masu albashi da suke da shi. Ku tattauna abin da zai faru ga isar da hidima idan yawan masu sa kai ya faɗi da kashi uku cikin shekara, tsarin da ba sabon abu ba bayan lokacin damuwar tattalin arziki ko katsewar lafiyar jama'a. Ku yi la'akari ko ƙungiyarku tana ɗaukar masu sa kai a matsayin masu haɗin gwiwar samarwa masu ainihin muradi a aikin (duba Batu 1.5 — Masu ruwa da tsaki, 'yan ƙasa da haɗin gwiwar samarwa) ko ƙari marar sarrafawa ga yawan ma'aikata.

**Shin hanyarmu ta yarjejeniyar ƙungiya ta gamayya tana gina dangantakar aiki mai ɗorewa, ko tana sake yin yaƙi ɗaya a kowane zagaye?** Ku bita zagayen yarjejeniyarku biyu ko uku na ƙarshe ku lura ko an warware su ta tattaunawa ta gaske, ta tayin ɗaya ɓangare da aka karɓa ƙarƙashin matsin lamba, ko ta tsangwama kamar yajin aiki ko aiki-bisa-ƙa'ida. Ku tattauna abin da yarjejeniyar tsari ta shekaru da yawa za ta ƙunsa don ta zama mai sahihanci ga ɓangarorin biyu, da abin da zai canza a yadda ƙungiyarku take shirya yarjejeniya — shiga da wuri, ingantattun bayanai, ikon da ke tsira daga sauyin siyasa — don isa can. Ku auna farashin wannan jari da farashin da ke maimaitawa na fuskantar juna na shekara-shekara.

## A aikace: misalin darajar jama'a

Wata hukumar kare muhalli ta ƙasa a wata ƙasa matsakaiciya ta shafe shekaru ashirin tana ɗaukar ma'aikata kusan gaba ɗaya ta hanyar tsarin sana'a na ma'aikatan gwamnati na gargajiya: masu kammala karatu suna shiga a matsayin ƙananan masu bincike, ana horar da su a cikin gida, kuma suna ɗagawa ta ƙayyadadden tsarin matakai cikin shekaru goma sha biyar zuwa ashirin. Samfurin ya samar da ma'aikata masu zurfin ilimin cibiya na tsarin ruwan ƙasar da ƙarfin ainihin ƙwararru na gamayya. Ya kuma samar da ma'aikata waɗanda matsakaicin shekarun manyan injiniyoyin ruwa a cikinsu ya kai hamsin da takwas, tare da kusan babu ɗaukar ma'aikata ta gefe na ƙwararrun ƙirƙirar samfura da na'urorin gano daga nesa da hukumar take buƙata yanzu don cika sababbin wajibai na sa ido kan yanayi.

Sashen shirin ma'aikata na hukumar ya yi samfurin haɗarin da tsananin: bisa tsarin ritaya na yanzu, kusan rabin injiniyoyin ruwa mafi ƙwarewa na hukumar za su bar cikin shekaru shida, kuma bututun masu kammala karatu da ya maye gurbin masu ritaya a baya cikin tabbaci ba zai iya samar da takamaiman ƙwarewar dijital da ƙirƙirar samfurin muhalli da sababbin ayyukan doka na hukumar suke buƙata ba, domin ba kasafai ake koyar da waɗannan ƙwarewa ba a hanyar shigar gama-gari da hukumar ta saba amfani da ita. Ɗaukar ma'aikata daga waje don manyan mukamai na ƙwararru zai nufin saita albashi sama da abin da ma'aikatan da aka ɗaga a cikin gida a daidai matakin suke samu, matakin da shugabancin hukumar ya guje wa tsawon shekaru saboda tsoron korafin cikin gida da sukar jama'a na "rashin daidaiton albashi ga daidai matakin".

Daraktar hukumar ta warware tashin hankalin ta hanyar yin shawarar a bayyane maimakon guje mata. Ta ƙirƙiri wata hanyar ƙwararru dabam, mai lakabi a fili, ta mukami ba ta sana'a ba, tare da nata ɗaurin albashi da aka ba da hujja a fili ta hanyar kasuwar aiki ta waje na masana kimiyyar bayanan muhalli, yayin da ta bar hanyar masu bincike ta sana'a da take akwai ba tare da taɓawa ba kuma ana mutunta ta daidai. Ta haɗa wannan da buƙatar canja wurin ilimi na shekaru biyu: kowane ƙwararre da aka ɗauka a sabuwar hanyar an ɗaure shi ta kwangila ya haɗa kai wajen isar da aƙalla manyan ayyuka biyu tare da babban injiniya mai ritaya kafin tafiyar wannan injiniya, yana mayar da shirin maye gurbi zuwa aiki da aka ba da kuɗi, aka tsara, maimakon fata. An yi shawarwarin canjin da ƙungiyar ƙwadago ta ɓangaren jama'a mai dacewa cikin watanni takwas, tare da amincewar ƙungiyar da aka samu a musayar alƙawarin cewa ba za a yi amfani da hanyar ƙwararru don kewaye damar ɗagawa a hanyar masu bincike da take akwai ba.

Bayan shekaru uku, hukumar ta cika mukaman ƙwararru da take buƙata, ta riƙe ƙarin rukunin masu ritayarta a matsayin masu ba da jagoranci na ɗan lokaci fiye da yadda tsarin da ta yi a baya ba bisa ƙa'ida ba ya cimma, kuma ta ba da rahoto — a cikin bitar cikin gida da ta bayyana a fili daga baya — cewa ɗabi'ar ma'aikata a hanyar masu bincike da take akwai ta tsaya daidai maimakon raguwa, abin da shugabancinta ya danganta kai tsaye ga yin shawarwarin canjin a buɗe maimakon sanar da shi a matsayin abin da aka riga aka yanke. Wannan yanayin misali ne kuma na almara; ba ya siffanta shirin ma'aikata na kowace hukumar muhalli ta gaske.

## Ra'ayoyin ɓangarori huɗu

### Ƙananan hukumomi

Ƙananan hukumomi yawanci suna gogayya don irin waɗannan sana'o'i — masu tsara birane, injiniyoyi, ma'aikatan zamantakewa, jami'an lafiyar muhalli — da hukumomin ƙasa da ɓangaren masu zaman kansu, sau da yawa da ƙarancin sassaucin albashi da ƙaramin yanki na ɗaukar ma'aikata, wanda ya sa tsarin mai saya ɗaya a wannan batu ya zama mai tsanani musamman ga ayyukan ƙwararru a ƙananan hukumomi ko masu nisa. Yarjejeniyar gamayya sau da yawa ta fi rarrabuwa a nan ma, ana kafa albashi a gida, a yanki, ko ta tsarin ƙasa da masu aiki na gida suke sarrafawa a wani ɓangare kawai, wanda yake bar shugabannin gida masu alhakin sakamakon da ba su kafa gaba ɗaya ba.

### Gwamnatin ƙasa

Gwamnatin ƙasa ita ce gidan gargajiya na ma'aikatan gwamnati na sana'a na ƙa'ida da kuma kafa albashi mafi bayyana a siyasa, domin ba da albashi na ƙasa ana ba da rahotonsa akai-akai, ana muhawara a kansa a majalisa, kuma gwamnati da adawa duka suna amfani da shi a matsayin sigina na muhimmancin kuɗi. Wannan ganuwa tana ba wa ma'aikatan gwamnati na ƙasa mafi ƙarfin dalili na amfani da hanyoyin bitar albashi masu zaman kansu da ƙa'idodin ɗaukar ma'aikata bisa cancanta da aka buga a fili, domin farashin suna na komawa da ake gani ga goyon baya na sirri ya fi girma inda amincewar jama'a ga gudanarwa mara ɓangaranci ta fi kai tsaye cikin hatsari (duba Batu 3.4 — Alhaki, bayyana gaskiya da halacci).

### Ɓangaren zamantakewa da marasa riba

Ƙungiyoyin ɓangaren zamantakewa da marasa riba suna sarrafa ma'aikata haɗaka na masu albashi da masu sa kai da wannan batu yake ɗauka a matsayin na musamman, kuma ƙwarin gwiwar hidimar jama'a sau da yawa a bayyane yake a yadda suke ɗaukar ma'aikata da bayyana manufarsu — ƙarfi na gaske, amma kuma fallasa ta gaske idan ya zama uzuri ga ƙarancin albashi na dindindin (duba Batu 3.8 — Gudanar da ɓangaren zamantakewa da ƙungiyoyi marasa riba don tsarin kuɗi da mulki da ke tsara abin da waɗannan ƙungiyoyi za su iya bayarwa ga ma'aikata). Shirin ma'aikata a nan dole ne kuma ya yi samfurin samar da masu sa kai, wanda yake kula da yanayin tattalin arziki da hankalin jama'a, tare da ma'aikata masu albashi — matsalar hasashe da yawancin kayan tsara ma'aikatan gwamnati ba a gina su don magancewa ba.

### Matakin ƙasashe da yawa da na ƙasa da ƙasa

Hukumomin ƙasashe da yawa da na ƙasa da ƙasa suna ɗaukar ma'aikata daga ma'aikatan gwamnati na ƙasashe da yawa da ɓangaren masu zaman kansu a lokaci guda, don haka dabarun sana'a da na mukami suna wanzuwa tare a ƙungiya ɗaya, sau da yawa suna samar da irin tashin hankalin cikin gida da hukumar ta almara da ke sama ta warware da gangan. Ƙayyade albashi yana ƙara rikitarwa ta buƙatar sikeli masu kwatankwacin ƙasa da ƙasa, da aka daidaita da kuɗin rayuwa a wuraren aiki da yawa, kuma yarjejeniyar gamayya, inda take wanzuwa, tana aiki ta ƙungiyoyin ma'aikata masu wakiltar ƙasashe da dama maimakon ƙungiyar ƙwadago ta ƙasa ɗaya, wanda ya sa yarjejeniyar tsari ta shekaru da yawa ta fi wuyar yin shawarwari kuma ta fi daraja idan an cimma.

## Hanyoyin gazawa na yau da kullum

**Matsewar albashi ƙarƙashin matsin siyasa.** Ministoci ko kwamitoci suna riƙe albashin manya da ƙwararru ƙasa don tsoron sukar kanun labarai game da albashin "attajirai", yayin da albashin ƙananan ma'aikata yake tashi ta hanyar yarjejeniya ta ƙara-ƙara, har sai giɓin albashi tsakanin ciki da waje ya yi girma isa ya tura ficewar ma'aikatan da suka fi ƙwarewa da ƙungiyar ta fi rashin iya rasawa. Asarar tana bayyana a hankali, a matsayin ɓarkewar ƙarfin fasaha na manya, maimakon rikici ɗaya a bayyane.

**Ɗaukar ƙwarin gwiwar hidimar jama'a a matsayin albarkatun kyauta.** Ƙungiya tana jingina da jajircewar ma'aikata ga manufa don ba da hujjar ƙarancin ma'aikata na dindindin ko albashi ƙasa da kasuwa, tana tunanin cewa mutanen da suke kula da aikin za su ɗauki matsin. Wannan yana aiki har sai ya daina: gajiya da ficewa suna cire ma'aikata masu mafi ƙwarin gwiwa da farko, domin su ne suka fi yiwuwa su sami wasu zaɓuɓɓuka kuma suka fi rashin son kallon manufa mai ƙarancin kayan aiki tana ci gaba har abada.

**Karkacewa ba tare da bayyana ba daga ɗaukar ma'aikata ta sana'a zuwa ta mukami.** Ƙungiyar da aka gina a kan ɗagawa ta cikin gida tana fara ɗaukar ma'aikata ta gefe don sauƙi, ba tare da sake tsara ƙa'idodin ɗagawa ko tsarin albashi don dacewa ba, tana samar da ma'aikata biyu masu aiki ƙarƙashin tsammanin da ba su dace ba a cibiya ɗaya. Ma'aikata masu tsawon hidima suna gane, sau da yawa daidai, cewa an canza ƙa'idodi ba tare da sake yin shawarwari ba, abin da ke jawo ƙorafi ko da kowace shawarar ɗaukar ma'aikata ana iya kare ta ɗaya-ɗaya.

**Gudanar da yarjejeniyar ƙungiya ta gamayya a matsayin sarrafa rikici na shekara-shekara.** Mai aiki yana yin shawarwarin albashi shekara ɗaya a lokaci, ƙarƙashin matsin kuɗi, ba tare da tsarin shekaru da yawa ba da kuma ƙarancin shiri, don haka kowane zagaye ya zama gasar fuskantar juna maimakon tattaunawa tsakanin ɓangarori masu ci gaba da dangantaka. Wannan yana cinye lokacin gudanarwa fiye da kima kuma yana ƙara yiwuwar aikin tsangwama kamar yajin aiki, wanda daga nan ya sa zagayen na gaba ya fi fuskantar juna.

**Shirin ma'aikata da yake ƙidayar kai kawai, ba ƙwarewa ko lokaci ba.** Ƙungiya tana bin diddigin jimillar yawan ma'aikata a kan kasafin kuɗi amma ba ta yi samfurin tsarin shekaru, haɗin ƙwarewa, ko tsarin ficewa a cikinsa ba, don haka dutsen alƙaluman jama'a a muhimmiyar sana'a ba a ganuwa a cikin jimillar lambobi har sai ya iso, a lokacin da lokacin jagorar da ake buƙata don horarwa ko ɗaukar maye gurbi yawanci ya riga ya ɓace.

## Jerin dubawa

- [ ] Manufar ɗaukar ma'aikata ta bayyana, a fili, waɗanne ayyuka na sana'a ne da waɗanne na mukami ne, da dalili.
- [ ] Ƙa'idodin ɗaukar ma'aikata bisa cancanta an buga su kuma ana amfani da su daidai, tare da naɗin siyasa a rabe a bayyane.
- [ ] Tsarin ƙayyade albashi, ciki har da kowace hanyar bita mai zaman kanta, an rubuta shi kuma ma'aikata sun fahimce shi.
- [ ] An gano ayyukan da ƙungiyar take mai aiki mai saya ɗaya, tare da auna albashi da mafi kyawun bayanan yanki da ake da su.
- [ ] Tsara lada da yabo yana nuna shaida kan ƙwarin gwiwar hidimar jama'a, ba zaton cewa albashi shi ne kaɗai abin ƙarfafawa ba.
- [ ] Shirin ma'aikata mai birgima yana ƙunsar tsarin shekaru, haɗin ƙwarewa, da haɗarin ficewa ga sana'o'i masu muhimmanci, ana sabunta shi aƙalla kowane shekara biyu zuwa uku.
- [ ] An ba da kuɗi kuma an tsara jadawalin shirye-shiryen maye gurbi da canja wurin ilimi ga ayyukan da ke fuskantar haɗarin ritaya na kusa.
- [ ] Haɗin kan masu sa kai, inda ake dogara da shi, nauyi ne da aka ba da kuɗi, aka ambata, ba ƙari ba bisa ƙa'ida ga nauyin aikin ma'aikata masu albashi ba.
- [ ] An kafa hanyar yarjejeniyar ƙungiya ta gamayya a tsarin shekaru da yawa inda kuɗi ya yarda, maimakon sake yin shawarwari daga farko kowace shekara.
- [ ] Ana binciken daidaiton albashi a matakin aiki, ba ta matsakaitan ƙungiya gaba ɗaya kaɗai ba.
- [ ] Manajojin gaba suna riƙe da ainihin ikon da aka ba su kayan aiki kan shawarwarin ɗaukar ma'aikata da riƙe su cikin shirin ma'aikata.
- [ ] An gaya wa ma'aikata a sarari abin da ƙungiya za ta iya kuma ba za ta iya alƙawari ba game da albashi, tsaro, da ci gaba.

## Manyan tushe

- Jerin *Government at a Glance* da *Public Employment and Management* na Ƙungiyar Haɗin Gwiwar Tattalin Arziki da Ci Gaba (OECD), don bayanan kwatance da nau'ikan aikin gwamnati, ɗaukar ma'aikata ta sana'a da ta mukami, da albashin ɓangaren jama'a a ƙasashen mambobi.
- Labarin James L. Perry da Lois Recascino Wise na 1990 a *Public Administration Review* da ya ƙirƙiri ƙwarin gwiwar hidimar jama'a, da shirin bincike da ya biyo baya, don tushen ka'ida da na zahiri na PSM a matsayin daban da ƙarfafawa ta kuɗi zalla.
- Yarjejeniyar Dangantakar Ƙwadago (Aikin Gwamnati), 1978 (Lamba 151) ta Ƙungiyar Ƙwadago ta Ƙasa da Ƙasa (ILO), da aikinta mai alaƙa na fannin aikin gwamnati, don tsarin ƙa'idodin ƙwadago na ƙasa da ƙasa na yarjejeniyar gamayya ta ɓangaren jama'a.
- Ƙa'idodin Ɗaukar Ma'aikata na Hukumar Ma'aikatan Gwamnati ta Birtaniya (Civil Service Commission Recruitment Principles), don misali da aka rubuta na yadda hukumar ma'aikatan gwamnati ta ƙasa take aiwatar da ɗaukar ma'aikata bisa cancanta a aikace.
- Nazarin shari'o'in tarihi na gyaran ma'aikatan gwamnati, musamman Dokar Gyaran Ma'aikatan Gwamnati ta Pendleton ta Amurka (1883) da Rahoton Northcote–Trevelyan na Birtaniya (1854), don asalin bambancin cancanta da goyon baya na sirri da har yanzu yake tsara ƙirar ma'aikatan gwamnati.
