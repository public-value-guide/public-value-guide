Summary: Public Value Guide is a handbook of best practices for creating public value in the social sector and government. It is written for leaders, elected and appointed government staff, nonprofit executive directors, and policy and program advisors who work with them. The guide is organized around Mark Moore's strategic triangle: legitimacy and support, the value proposition, and operational capacity. 

Benefits: You get a clear consistent working vocabulary for budgets, plus proven tools. Build a cost-benefit case. Design a scorecard that resists gaming. Run a crisis response well. Negotiate a fair contract with a supplier. Each topic stands on its own, so a reader facing a decision can go directly to relevant guidance.

Why it matters: Public-sector and social-sector leaders spend collective resources under scrutiny. Most management theory is written for a shareholder audience instead. This project treats public value as its own discipline, with its own tools, failure patterns, and maturity curve. A public manager deserves that same rigor, and a genuinely public case instead of a borrowed private one.
