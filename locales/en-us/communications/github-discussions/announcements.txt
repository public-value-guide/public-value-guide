Welcome to Public Value Guide Discussions

Public Value Guide now has GitHub Discussions, a place to ask questions, suggest ideas, and share what you have done with it.

Public Value Guide is a practical handbook of best practices for creating public value in government and the social sector, built on Mark Moore's strategic triangle. It is written for agency heads, local-government leaders, nonprofit and social-enterprise executives, policy and programme directors, and the digital, finance, and operations leaders who serve them.

## What this means for you

- You can ask questions and get answers from other readers.
- You can suggest topics, fixes, and improvements before anyone writes them.
- You can share how you use the guide.

## Where to go for what

- Questions about using the guide: Q&A
- Suggestions for new or changed topics: Ideas
- Quick community votes: Polls
- Things you have built or tried with the guide: Show and tell
- Everything else: General
- Errors, broken links, or typos: Issues (https://github.com/public-value-guide/public-value-guide/issues)
