Say hello and tell us how you use Public Value Guide

Welcome to the Public Value Guide Discussions. This is the place for introductions, opinions, and open conversation about the guide.

I am a maintainer of the project. I wrote Public Value Guide because I wanted a practical handbook of best practices for creating public value in government and the social sector, built on Mark Moore's strategic triangle to be easy to find and use. I would like to hear who is reading it and why.

**One question to start:** How do you explain the value of your organization's work to people outside it?

A sentence or two is plenty. Replies to other people are welcome too.
