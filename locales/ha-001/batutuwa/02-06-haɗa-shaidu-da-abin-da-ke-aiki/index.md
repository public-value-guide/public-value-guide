# Batu 2.6 — Haɗa shaidu da abin da ke aiki

**Bincike ɗaya bayani ne guda ɗaya, ba hukunci ba; ƙa'idar haɗa shaidu tana mayar da bayanai da yawa marasa cikawa zuwa hukunci mai tushe game da abin da zai iya aiki, kuma aikin shugaban jama'a shi ne ya yi amfani da wannan hukunci ba tare da ɗaukar matakan shaida a matsayin lasisin jira ba.**

## Dalilin da ya sa wannan yake da muhimmanci ga darajar jama'a

Minista, magajin gari, ko daraktan shirin ƙungiyar sadaka ba kasafai yake da dama ta bincike guda, tsabta, da zai yi aiki da ita ba. Sau da yawa teburin yana ɗauke da kimantawa uku da suka saɓa wa juna, bitar adabi da ke ambaton su zaɓaɓɓu, ƙungiyar matsi tana ɗaga gwajin da ya dace da shari'arta, da wa'adin da zagayen kasafin kuɗi ya tsara maimakon saurin bincike. Haɗa shaidu yana wanzuwa ne saboda binciken daidaiku yana da hayaniya: samfuran ƙanana ne, yanayi ya bambanta, kuma kowane sakamako zai iya zama kwatsam, dacewar gida da ba za ta yi tafiya ba, ko tasiri na gaske. Tambayar da shugaba yake buƙatar amsa ba kasafai ita ce "me wannan binciken ya gano?" ba amma "a cikin duk abin da aka gwada da za a iya amincewa da shi, me zan yi tsammani idan na yi wannan a nan, yanzu?"

Ka'idar darajar jama'a tana mayar da wannan fiye da ƙyalli na fasaha. Shirin da aka ba da kuɗi da albarkatun gamayya yana tsaye a kan shawarar daraja da 'yan ƙasa, a jimilla, za su ba da izini (duba Batu 2.1 — Katin ƙididdigar darajar jama'a da tsarin sakamako don yadda ake bayyana da bin diddigin wannan shawara). Shawarar da aka gina a kan binciken da aka zaɓa ɗaya, ko ba tare da wata bitar shaida ba, iƙirari ne mafi rauni ga halacci fiye da wadda aka gina a kan haɗin shaida mai kulawa — kuma tushe mafi rauni ga ƙarfin aiki, domin ana ɗaukar ma'aikata da kasafin kuɗi bisa ƙarfinta. Ƙungiyar "abin da ke aiki" — hanyar sadarwar hukumomi masu zaman kansu da ke tantance inganci da ƙarfin shaidar manufa — ta tashi kai-tsaye daga wannan matsala: an yanke shawarwari da yawa na jama'a bisa binciken da ya fi ƙara ƙara maimakon wanda ya fi samun goyon baya.

Matsalar mafi wahala da wannan batu yake magana a kai ita ce abin da shugaba yake yi idan haɗin shaidar kansa bai tabbata ba, ana jayayya a kai, ko kuma ba ya samuwa a kan lokaci. An gina matakan shaida don kawo tsari ga tushen shaida mai ruɗani, amma idan aka yi amfani da su ba tare da kulawa ba, "shaidar ba ta isa ba tukuna" tana zama uzuri na dindindin na guje wa shawarar da gaskiya ba za ta jira ba. Shugaba da ya fahimci yadda haɗa shaida yake aiki, da inda iyakokinsa suke, zai iya bambanta rashin tabbas na gaske da ke ba da dalilin taka-tsantsan da matakin shaida da ake amfani da shi a matsayin rufin rashin aiki.

Wannan batu shi ne ci gaba na dabi'a na hanyoyin nazarin dalili da haɗa shaida ke ɗauka daga gare su (duba Batu 2.4 — Ilimin auna tattalin arziki na ɓangaren jama'a da kimanta shirye-shirye) kuma yana tsaye gefe da gefe, ba yana maye gurbin ba, tsare-tsaren sakamako da haɗa shaida ke yi wa hidima a ƙarshe (duba Batu 2.1 — Katin ƙididdigar darajar jama'a da tsarin sakamako). Shugaba da zai iya karanta bitar tsari, ya fahimci ƙimar abin da ke aiki, kuma ya yi tunani a fili game da shaidar da ba ta nan da shaidar mara kyau yana yin shawarwari masu kyau na zahiri fiye da wanda zai iya neman "ƙarin shaida" kawai ko ya karɓi binciken farko da aka ba shi.

## Muhimman ra'ayoyi

**Bitar tsari (systematic review)** haɗin shaida ne kan tambaya da aka ayyana wanda yake amfani da hanya a bayyane, da aka fayyace tun da wuri, wadda za a iya maimaitawa don nemowa, tantancewa da haɗa bincike, bambanci da bitar adabi ta labari, wadda take zaɓar tushe bisa ra'ayin marubuci (duba [Bitar tsari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systematic_review)). Siffofinta masu ayyanawa su ne tsarin aiki da aka yi rajista kafin a fara bincike, dabarar bincike da ta ƙunshi adabin da ba a wallafa ba da na toka, ka'idojin shigarwa a bayyane, da hanya ta daidaito don tantance haɗarin karkata na kowane bincike. Wannan shi ne ke bambanta bitar tsari da kyakkyawan rubutu mai bayanan ƙasa: wata ƙungiya da ta bi tsarin aiki iri ɗaya za ta sami kusan shaida iri ɗaya kuma ta kai kusan ƙarshe iri ɗaya.

**Nazarin haɗaɗɗen sakamako (meta-analysis)** shi ne dabarar ƙididdiga da aka fi haɗawa da bitar tsari: yana tattara girman tasirin ƙididdiga daga bincike masu zaman kansu da yawa zuwa kiyasi guda, mafi daidai, yawanci yana auna kowane bincike bisa girman samfurinsa da ingancinsa (duba [Nazarin haɗaɗɗen sakamako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta-analysis)). Nazarin haɗaɗɗen sakamako yana da kyau ne kawai kamar binciken da ke ciyar da shi; tara bincike biyar masu rauni yana samar da kiyasi mafi daidai na tasirin da wataƙila ya karkata, ba mai abin dogaro ba. Don haka masu nazari suna ba da rahoton **bambancin bincike (heterogeneity)** — yawan sakamakon bincike ya bambanta fiye da yadda kwatsam zai yi hasashe — domin babban bambanci yana nuna cewa binciken ba da gaske suke gwada abu ɗaya ba, kuma lamba ɗaya da aka tara na iya yaudara.

**Karkatar wallafawa (publication bias)** shi ne yanayin da binciken da ke da sakamako mai kyau, mai muhimmanci ake wallafa shi yayin da sakamakon babu komai ko marasa kyau suke zaune a aljihun mai bincike, yana sa tsoma baki ya yi kama da mai tasiri da daidaito fiye da yadda yake a zahiri a cikin adabi (duba [Karkatar wallafawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publication_bias)). Bita mai tsauri tana gyara wannan ta hanyar bincika rajistar gwaje-gwaje da kimantawa da ba a wallafa ba, ba kawai labaran mujallu ba. Shugaba da ke karanta haɗin shaida da aka gina kan takardun da aka wallafa kawai ya kamata ya tambayi abin da masu bita suka yi don nemo binciken da bai taɓa shiga bugu ba.

**Matakan shaida (hierarchy of evidence)** suna jera ƙirar bincike bisa yadda suke sarrafa haɗewa da karkata, yawanci suna sanya nazarin haɗaɗɗen sakamako na gwaje-gwajen bazuwar a sama, gwaje-gwajen bazuwar masu sarrafawa daidaiku a gaba, sannan ƙirar kusa-da-gwaji da ta lura, da ra'ayin ƙwararru a ƙasa (duba [Matakan shaida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hierarchy_of_evidence)). **Gwajin bazuwar mai sarrafawa (randomized controlled trial)** yana ba da mahalarta ga tsoma baki ko ƙungiyar kwatance bisa kwatsam, kayan aiki mafi ƙarfi guda don raba tasirin dalili daga duk abin da ke faruwa a kewaye da shi (duba [Gwajin bazuwar mai sarrafawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randomized_controlled_trial); duba Batu 2.4 — Ilimin auna tattalin arziki na ɓangaren jama'a da kimanta shirye-shirye don yadda ake gina da nazarin irin waɗannan ƙira). Matakan ƙa'ida ce mai amfani da gaske, amma ƙa'ida ce, ba doka ba: kyakkyawan binciken lura na manufa da ba kasafai ake gani ba ko mai jinkirin motsi na iya zarce gwaji da aka gudanar da rauni, mai rauni na ƙarfi, kuma shugaba da yake ɗaukar matakan a matsayin cikakken jeri zai wani lokaci ya yar da shaida mafi dacewa don ƙirar da ta fi suna.

**Hanyar GRADE** — Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development and Evaluations — tana tsara hukunci na biyu, daban da matakan shaida kaɗai ba sa yi: tana ƙididdige duka tabbacin shaida da, ba tare da juna ba, ƙarfin shawarar yin aiki da ita, domin ƙarfin shaida ba koyaushe yana tabbatar da ƙarfin shawara ba, kuma raunin shaida ba koyaushe yana tabbatar da rashin aiki a kan mummunar cuta mai gaggawa ba (duba [Hanyar GRADE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GRADE_approach)). Wannan rabuwa ita ce mafi amfani ra'ayi guda a wannan batu ga mai yanke shawara da lokaci ya matse: yana ba shugaba damar cewa, da gaskiya, "shaidar a nan matsakaiciya ce, kuma bisa abin da ke kan layi da kuɗin jira, ina ba da shawarar mu ci gaba" — hukunci mai kariya, da aka rubuta, maimakon zaɓi na ƙarya tsakanin jiran tabbaci da yin watsi da shaida gaba ɗaya.

Bambanci da ke maimaituwa koyaushe a muhawarar manufa ta zahiri shi ne tsakanin **rashin shaida (absence of evidence)** da **shaidar rashi (evidence of absence)**: rashin bincike kan tasirin tsoma baki ("babu shaidar tasiri") ba iƙirari ɗaya ba ne da tarin bincike masu ƙarfi da suka gano rashin tasiri da gaske ("shaidar rashin tasiri") (duba [Shaidar rashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evidence_of_absence)). Sabon shirin gida da ba shi da kimantawa tukuna ba a gwada shi ba kawai; shirin ƙasa da aka yi nazari sosai wanda aka maimaita gano ba ya motsa wani sakamako an gwada shi kuma an nuna ba ya aiki. Cakuɗa su biyun ɗaya ne daga cikin kuskure mafi sakamako da shugaban jama'a zai iya yi: kashe gwaji mai alƙawari wanda ba a gwada ba kamar dai ya kasa, ko ci gaba da ba da kuɗin shirin da aka maimaita karyatawa kamar dai babu wanda ya duba sosai tukuna.

**Manufa bisa shaida (evidence-based policy)** ita ce ƙungiya mafi faɗi da iƙirari na ɗabi'a cewa shawarwarin jama'a su kasance a kan shaida da aka kafa da tsauri maimakon akida, labari, ko ilhama kaɗai (duba [Manufa bisa shaida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evidence-based_policy)). Masu sukarta, da kuma masu goyon bayanta da ke ƙaruwa, sun fi son "manufa da shaida ta sanar", wadda ta yarda cewa shaida shigarwa ɗaya ce cikin da yawa — dabi'u, umarni, kuɗi, da tambayoyin halacci da wannan littafi yake ɗauka a matsayin tushe — maimakon ƙa'idar yanke shawara ta inji da shaida kaɗai za ta iya warwarewa.

Daga wannan ƙungiya ta tashi tsarin **cibiyar abin da ke aiki ko ma'ajiyar share (what-works center or clearinghouse)**: hukuma mai zaman kanta da take duba shaidar tsoma baki a fannin manufa bisa tsari kuma tana wallafa ƙimomi masu sauƙin samu, masu kwatantuwa, maimakon barin kowane mai bayar da kwangila na gida ya gudanar da nasa bita. **Cochrane** ta fara samfurin a kiwon lafiya tun 1993, tana kula da littafin jagora mai rai na hanyar bitar tsari da ake amfani da shi fiye da likitanci (duba [Cochrane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cochrane_(organisation))). **Campbell Collaboration** ta faɗaɗa hanya ɗaya zuwa manufofin zamantakewa, shari'ar laifuka, ilimi, da ci gaban ƙasa da ƙasa (duba [Campbell Collaboration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campbell_Collaboration)). Ma'ajiyoyin share na ƙasa sun biyo baya: **What Works Clearinghouse** na Amurka, wanda Cibiyar Kimiyyar Ilimi (Institute of Education Sciences) take gudanarwa tun 2002, tana duba binciken ilimi bisa ma'aunin ƙira a bayyane (duba [What Works Clearinghouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_Works_Clearinghouse)); **Education Endowment Foundation** ta Ingila tana wallafa Teaching and Learning Toolkit da ake amfani da shi sosai wanda yake ƙididdige tsoma bakin aji bisa kuɗi, ƙarfin shaida, da tasirin da aka saba a tsari ɗaya mai kwatantuwa (duba [Education Endowment Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_Endowment_Foundation)). Cibiyoyi makamantansu suna wanzuwa don rage aikata laifi, tsoma baki da wuri, rashin matsuguni, da tsufa cikin koshin lafiya — ƙaramin iyali na ƙasa da ƙasa da aka gina a kan hanya ɗaya: bitar tsari, ƙima a bayyane, da taƙaitaccen bayani na masu aiki wanda ba ya buƙatar masanin hanya ya karanta shi.

## Ingantattun ayyuka

1. **Ku nemi haɗin shaida kafin bincike guda.** Idan sakamako mai ban mamaki ya sauka a teburinku, ku fara tambaya ko bitar tsari ko ƙimar abin da ke aiki ta riga ta wanzu ga wannan tsoma baki, ba ko binciken yana da gamsarwa shi kaɗai ba.
2. **Ku karanta bitar da ke ƙarƙashi tare da kowace ƙimar kayan aiki, ba maimakonta ba.** Ƙima ta layi ɗaya ko alamar makulli taƙaitaccen hukunci ne; ba madadin fahimtar shaidar da take dogara a kai da yadda take shafar yanayinku kai-tsaye ba.
3. **Ku raba ingancin shaida daga ƙarfin shawararku a fili.** Ku aro dabarar GRADE ko da ba bisa ƙa'ida ba: ku faɗi yadda shaidar take da tabbaci, sannan ku faɗi daban yadda wannan tabbaci, tare da abin da ke kan layi da kuɗi, ke tabbatar da yin aiki yanzu.
4. **Ku bayyana ko kuna fuskantar "babu shaida tukuna" ko "shaidar rashin tasiri" kafin ku yanke shawara.** Ku rubuta wannan bambanci a kowane taƙaitaccen shaida, domin yanayi biyun suna buƙatar martani kusan akasin juna — gwaji mai taka-tsantsan da dakatarwa ko sake ƙira.
5. **Ku duba dacewar yanayin bita da naku kafin ku zaci tasirin zai canja.** Girman tasirin da aka tara daga bincike da aka yi a wani wuri matsakaici ne, ba alƙawari ba; ku tambaya abin da ya daidaita tasirin a asali da ko waɗannan masu daidaitawa suna nan a nan.
6. **Ku ba da kimantawar shaida cikin sauri idan cikakkiyar bitar tsari ba za ta shiga jadawalinku ba.** Bitar da aka tsara, mai iyakacin lokaci, na adabin da ke akwai — kwanaki ko makonni, ba watanni ba — hanya ce ta tsakiya mai halacci tsakanin sabon bincike na farko da rashin bita kwata-kwata.
7. **Ku wallafa kimantawarku ba tare da la'akari da sakamako ba.** Sakamakon babu komai da marasa kyau suna da daraja ga haɗin shaida na gaba kamar masu kyau; riƙe su yana ta'azzara karkatar wallafawa da ke lalata kowace bita ta gaba a fannin ku.
8. **Ku gina alaƙa ta dindindin da cibiyar abin da ke aiki ko ma'ajiyar share a fannin ku kafin ku buƙace ta.** Bincike a sanyi a cikin kayan aikin hukumar haɗin shaida a karon farko a lokacin rikici na zahiri ya fi jinkiri fiye da sanin abin da take rufewa da wanda za a tambaya game da gibi.
9. **Ku saita ƙofar shaidarku kafin sakamako ya iso, ba bayansa ba.** Ku amince a gaba wane matakin shaida zai tabbatar da faɗaɗawa, ƙarin gwaji, ko dakatarwa, don rage jarabar sake fassara sakamako maras tabbas don dacewa da shawarar da aka riga aka yanke.
10. **Ku iyakance lokacin neman binciken da zai kawar da duk shakka.** Jiran cikakkiyar tushen shaida mara shakka kansa shawara ce, yawanci ta ci gaba da duk abin da kuke yi tuni; ku saita wa'adi da ke girmama kuɗin jinkiri da kuɗin yin aiki bisa shaida mai rauni.
11. **Ku ɗauki haɗin shaida a matsayin shigarwa ga shawarar daraja, ba maye gurbinta ba.** Tsoma bakin da shaida ta fi goyon baya ba lallai ne zaɓin da ya dace ba da zarar an auna kuɗi, tasirin rabo, da halacci tare da shi (duba Batu 2.1 — Katin ƙididdigar darajar jama'a da tsarin sakamako).
12. **Ku rubuta shaidar da kuka yi la'akari da ita, da dalilin da ya sa kuka ba ta nauyi haka.** Rubutaccen bayanin binciken da aka duba da dalilin hukuncin ƙarshe yana kare halaccin shawarar da kuma ikon mai bita na gaba na koyo daga gare ta.

## Tambayoyi don tattaunawa da ƙungiya

**Yaushe ne lokacin ƙarshe da muka canza shawara ta zahiri saboda bitar tsari ko ƙimar abin da ke aiki ta saɓa wa abin da muka riga muka shirya yi?** Idan amsar gaskiya ita ce "ba mu taɓa ba", wannan ya cancanci tunani, domin yana nuna cewa bitar shaida na iya aiki a matsayin motsa jiki na tabbatarwa maimakon bincike na gaske. Ku tattauna wata shawara ta kwanan nan inda tushen shaida ya haɗu kuma ku bi ko ya tsara zaɓin da gaske ko an tattara shi musamman don goyon bayan ƙarshe da aka cimma a baya. Ku yi la'akari da abin da zai buƙaci sauyi a yadda shaida ke isa ga masu yanke shawara, da lokacin, don ya sami tasiri na gaske maimakon nauyi na alama.

**Shin muna ɗaukar "babu shaidar tasiri" kamar "shaidar rashin tasiri" a ko'ina, ko akasin haka?** Ku bi jerin shirye-shiryenku na yanzu ku gano duk shirin da kuka rage fifiko a ɓoye saboda "shaidar ba ta nan", ku duba ko hakan yana nufin ba a gwada shi ba da gaske ko an karyata shi da gaske. Ku yi haka a wata hanya: duk shirin da kuke ci gaba da ba da kuɗi saboda "babu wanda ya nuna cewa ba ya aiki", alhali a zahiri babu wanda ya duba. Ku tattauna abin da zai ɗauka don gyara misalan biyu ko uku mafi sakamako, da wanda ya kamata ya mallaki wannan gyara.

**Idan shawara tana buƙatar shaida a ranar Juma'a, shin muna da tsarin kimantawar shaida cikin sauri da gaske, ko za mu yi ɗaya ba tare da shiri ba a ƙarƙashin matsi?** Matsin lokaci ba hasashe ba ne; shi ne yanayin al'ada da ake yanke yawancin shawarwari na gaske bisa shaida. Ku tattauna ko ƙungiyarku tana da hanya ta dindindin — ƙungiya mai suna, samfuri, ɗan gajeren jerin cibiyoyin abin da ke aiki da za a duba da farko — ko kowace buƙata mai gaggawa tana farawa daga shafi mara komai. Idan na ƙarshe, ku tattauna yadda mafi ƙarancin tsarin kimantawa mai aiki zai yi kama da wanda zai mallake shi.

**Matakan shaidar wa muke amfani da su a zahiri, kuma shin sun dace da abin da ke kan layi da yiwuwar komawa baya na wannan shawara?** Fannoni daban-daban suna amfani da ma'aunin shaidarsu daban, kuma matakan da aka daidaita don shawarwari marasa komawa baya, masu girman sakamako ba koyaushe ne daidai ma'auni ga gwaji mai arha, mai komawa baya, mai sauƙin sa ido ba. Ku tattauna ko ƙungiyarku tana amfani da ma'aunin shaida ɗaya ba tare da la'akari da abin da ke kan layi ba, da ko wannan yana kare ku daga shawarwari marasa kyau ko kawai yana jinkirta masu kyau. Ku amince, a matsayin ƙungiya, wane ma'auni ya kamata a buƙaci ga shawarwari uku na gaba a cikin ajandarku, kafin ku san abin da shaidar ta ce.

**Me muka yi, a zahiri, don hana kimantawarmu ɓacewa cikin aljihun fayil?** Duk sakamakon babu komai da ba a wallafa ba da ƙungiyarku take zaune a kai gibi ne da wata ƙungiya za ta sake gano ta hanya mai wahala. Ku tattauna tarihinku da gaskiya: kimantawa nawa daga cikin 10 na ƙarshe aka raba a waje, da abin da ya faru da waɗanda ba a raba ba. Ku amince da alƙawari na zahiri, mai albarkatu — manufar wallafawa, rajista da aka raba — maimakon buri na gabaɗaya na kasancewa mai bayyana gaskiya.

**Shin muna amfani da kalmar "muna buƙatar ƙarin shaida" da gaskiya, ko a matsayin hanyar guje wa shawarar da ba ma son yankewa?** Wannan ita ce tambaya mafi rashin daɗi a wannan batu, kuma tana cancanta a yi ta kai-tsaye. Ku gano wata takamaiman shawara da ta tsaya a kan dalilan shaida a yanzu ku tattauna, a fili, abin da zai canza ra'ayinku a zahiri, da ko shaidar da kuke jira za ta iso a kan lokaci. Idan ba za ta iso ba, ku tattauna yadda shawara ta gaskiya a ƙarƙashin rashin tabbas na gaske za ta yi kama maimakon ci gaba da jira, da wanda yake da iko ya yanke ta.

## A aikace: misalin darajar jama'a

Wata hukumar aikin yanki, wani ɓangare na ma'aikatar gwamnatin ƙasa mai alhakin shirye-shiryen kasuwar aiki, tana ƙarƙashin matsi ta yanke shawara cikin zagayen kasafin kuɗi ɗaya ko ta faɗaɗa gwajin horon aiki daga gundumomi uku zuwa yankin gabaɗaya. Gwajin ya haɗa wuraren aiki masu tallafi da jagoranci mai tsari, kuma gwajin bazuwar na hukumar kanta a gundumomi uku na gwajin ya gano ƙaruwar aiki mai ɗorewa a watanni 12, ƙarama amma mai muhimmanci a ƙididdiga. Wata tushen shaida mai gogayya ta dagula hoton: bitar tsari da aka wallafa shekarar da ta gabata, tana tara shirye-shirye tara masu kama a ƙasashe da yawa, ta sami matsakaicin tasiri kusan sifili, tare da babban bambanci tsakanin bincike kuma babu bayyanannen tsari da ke bayyana waɗanne shirye-shirye ne suka yi aiki.

Sashen shaida na hukumar yana da makonni shida kafin wa'adin miƙa kasafin kuɗi don daidaita binciken biyun. Ilhamar farko ta ƙaramin mai nazari ita ce ɗaukar bitar da aka tara a matsayin mafi iko kawai saboda ta haɗa bincike da yawa, kuma ta ba da shawarar soke gwajin. Babban mai ba da shawara kan shaida na sashen ya ƙalubalanta: matsakaicin kusan sifili da aka tara a tsakanin shirye-shirye daban-daban da gaske ba shaida mai ƙarfi ba ce cewa wannan takamaiman shirin gida, da aka aiwatar da kyau, zai kasa — shaida ce cewa shirye-shiryen horon aiki gabaɗaya sun bambanta a inganci da dacewa, wanda aka riga aka sani. Babban bambancin bincike da bitar ta bayar shi ne ainihin binciken da ya fi muhimmanci fiye da matsakaicin da aka tara.

Mai ba da shawarar ta ba da shawarar hanyar tsakiya maimakon zaɓi biyu. Ta ba da shawarar faɗaɗawa a matakai zuwa rabin yankin, tare da gundumomin faɗaɗawa da aka zaɓa bazuwar don ƙaddamarwar kanta ta samar da ƙarin shaida maimakon kashe kuɗi kawai bisa zato (duba Batu 2.4 — Ilimin auna tattalin arziki na ɓangaren jama'a da kimanta shirye-shirye don yadda za a iya tsara ƙaddamarwa a matakai a matsayin ƙarin kimantawa). Ta kuma ba da shawarar wallafa gwajin na asali gabaɗaya, hanyoyi da bayanan ɗanye da su, don bitar tsari ta gaba ta ɗauke shi maimakon ya zauna a rahoton cikin gida da babu wanda ke wajen hukumar zai taɓa gani.

Shugabancin ma'aikatar ya amince da hanyar matakai, wanda aka tsara a fili a cikin hujjar kasuwanci (duba Batu 2.5 — Hujjojin kasuwanci da darajar kuɗi) a matsayin shawara da aka yanke a ƙarƙashin shaida ta matsakaicin tabbaci tare da hanyar da aka gina don warware rashin tabbas cikin watanni 18, ba a matsayin shawara da aka yanke saboda shaida ta kasance ta ƙarshe ba. Bayan watanni 18, gundumomin faɗaɗawa sun nuna tasiri kusa da na gwajin na asali, kuma ma'aikatar ta faɗaɗa gaba da tushen shaida mafi ƙarfi fiye da kowane tushe ɗaya da ya bayar shi kaɗai — da bayyananniyar hanyar takarda da ke nuna dalilin da ya sa ta zaɓi yin aiki maimakon jira.

Wannan yanayin misali ne na ƙirƙira; ba ya bayyana shirin ko kimantawar kowace ma'aikata ta gaske.

## Ra'ayoyin ɓangarori huɗu

### Ƙananan hukumomi

Ƙaramar hukuma ba kasafai take da kasafin kuɗi ko yawan shari'o'i don ba da umarnin bitar tsarinta ba, don haka fasaharta ta aiki ita ce fassara shaidar haɗa da ta ƙasa da ta ƙasa da ƙasa zuwa takamaiman yanayin gida a ƙarƙashin matsin lokaci na gaske — duba ko tushen shaidar tsoma bakin da kayan aiki ya ƙima ya yi kama da jama'arta, tsarin hidima, da matakin kuɗinta kafin zaton cewa tasirin da aka ƙima zai canja. Shugabannin gida kuma suna kusa da kuɗin kuskuren biyun da wannan batu yake gargaɗi a kai: yin aiki da tsoma bakin da haɗin shaida ya riga ya nuna ba ya aiki, da ƙin sabon abu na gida mai alƙawari na gaske amma ba a kimanta shi sosai ba saboda babu cibiyar abin da ke aiki da ta ƙima shi tukuna.

### Gwamnatin ƙasa

Gwamnatin ƙasa ita ce inda yawancin cibiyoyin abin da ke aiki da jagorar matakan shaida ta hukuma suke, wanda ke ba ma'aikatun ƙasa kusanci da kayan aikin haɗa shaida amma kuma babban fallasa ga ɗaukar matakan shaida a matsayin ƙofa ta ofis maimakon taimakon yanke shawara. Aikin ƙasa na musamman shi ne ba da umarni da kula da wannan kayan aiki — ba da kuɗin ma'ajiyar share, saita ma'aunin shaidarta, da tabbatar da cewa ƙimominta suna isa ga jami'an da ke rubuta hujjojin kasuwanci (duba Batu 2.5 — Hujjojin kasuwanci da darajar kuɗi), maimakon kasancewa gidan yanar gizo mai daraja da ƙungiyoyin manufa ba kasafai suke dubawa ba a ƙarƙashin matsin wa'adi.

### Ɓangaren zamantakewa da marasa riba

Ƙungiyoyi marasa riba da na sadaka yawanci ba su da albarkatu don gudanar da kimantawa masu tsauri a ma'auni, wanda ke sa su dogara sosai ga hukumomin haɗin shaida na waje, kuma ƙara dogaro ga masu bayar da kuɗi da ke buƙatar daidaitawa da matakin shaida da aka ƙima a matsayin sharaɗin tallafi. Wannan yana haifar da haɗari na musamman: ƙungiyoyi a ƙarƙashin matsin kuɗi na iya jarabtuwa da wuce gona da iri kan yadda shirinsu ya dace da ƙima mai kyau — nau'in wanke shaida — ko, akasin haka, ƙananan ƙungiyoyin sadaka na gida masu tasiri da gaske za a iya matsa su daga kuɗi saboda ba su taɓa girma da isa don samar da shaidar da ma'ajiyar share ta ƙasa ke ƙimanta sosai ba (duba Batu 3.8 — Gudanar da ɓangaren zamantakewa da ƙungiyoyi marasa riba don tsarin kuɗi da ke bayan wannan matsi).

### Matakin ƙasashe da yawa da na ƙasa da ƙasa

Hukumomin ƙasashe da yawa da na ƙasa da ƙasa suna fuskantar matsalar yaɗuwa a mafi tsananinta: tsoma bakin da aka tabbatar a tsarin kiwon lafiyar ƙasa ɗaya, ɓangaren noma, ko kasuwar aiki ana ba da shawara a kai kullum ga yanayin tattalin arziki da cibiyoyi mabambanta, kuma adabin haɗa shaida ya fi kyau wajen nuna wannan canja da ba a tabbatar ba bayan faruwa fiye da kafin. Cibiyoyin bincike da aka gina don rufe wannan gibi, kamar Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab, sun tura manufar ci gaba zuwa maimaita da ya dace da yanayi maimakon tabbacin ra'ayi a ƙasa ɗaya, amma tashin hankali tsakanin shaidar da ake iya kwatantawa a duniya da tasirin da ya dogara da yanayi ya ci gaba da zama siffa mai ayyanawa ta haɗa shaida a wannan ma'auni.

## Hanyoyin gazawa na yau da kullum

**Matakan shaida a matsayin uzurin rashin aiki.** Ƙungiya da ke fuskantar shawara mai wahala, mai jayayya tana ambaton "rashin isasshiyar shaida" ko "babu gwajin bazuwar tukuna" a matsayin dalilin jinkiri na har abada, alhali ainihin dalilin jinkirin rashin jin daɗin siyasa ne ko sha'awar guje wa alhaki. Wannan yana kashe ƙungiyar damar kanta, kuma yana kashe tushen shaida gwajin zahiri da zai iya warware rashin tabbas, yana barin mai yanke shawara na gaba yana fuskantar daidai gibi ɗaya.

**Cakuɗa rashin shaida da shaidar rashi.** Ana kashe sabon abu na gida mai alƙawari wanda ba a gwada ba kamar dai an gwada shi kuma ya kasa, yayin da shirin ƙasa da aka maimaita karyatawa ake ci gaba da ba shi kuɗi domin babu wanda ya tsara shaidar a matsayin bincike mara kyau maimakon gibi. Kuskuren biyun suna da tsada: ɗaya yana rufe zaɓuɓɓuka masu alƙawari, ɗayan yana kare shirye-shiryen da haɗin shaida mai hangen nesa zai yi ritaya shekaru da yawa a baya.

**Ɗaukar ƙimar kayan aiki a matsayin cikakken nazari.** Mai yanke shawara yana karanta alamar makulli ɗaya ko ƙimar taurari ya tsaya a nan, ba tare da duba ko binciken da ke ƙarƙashi ya dace da yanayinsa, jama'arsa, ko ma'aunin sa ba. Ana matse ƙima da gangan don samun sauƙi; idan aka yi amfani da su ba tare da bitar da ke ƙarƙashi ba, za su iya samar da shawarwari masu ƙarfin gwiwa da aka gina a kan tushen shaida mafi siriri fiye da yadda sauƙin ƙimar yake nunawa.

**Yin watsi da bambancin bincike don goyon bayan sakamakon da aka tara na kan gaba.** Ana ambaton girman tasirin matsakaici guda na nazarin haɗaɗɗen sakamako a cikin taƙaitaccen bayani yayin da ake jefar da babban bambanci tsakanin binciken da suka samar da shi — sau da yawa binciken mafi bayani. Wannan yana samar da daidaito na ƙarya: mai yanke shawara yana aiki kamar kowace aiwatarwa tana nuna hali iri ɗaya, alhali ingancin aiwatarwa da yanayi suna yin aiki mai yawa kamar tsoma bakin kansa.

**Barin karkatar wallafawa ta tsara tushen shaida a hankali.** Ƙungiya tana karanta adabin ilimi da aka wallafa kawai lokacin da take tantance tsoma baki, tana rasa gwaje-gwaje marasa kyau da ba a wallafa ba da kimantawa na cikin gida da ba su taɓa shiga mujallu ba. Hoton "abin da ke aiki" da ya haifar yana karkata zuwa mai kyau bisa tsari, kuma shawarwarin da aka gina a kansa suna gadon wannan karkata ba tare da wani ya zaɓe ta da gangan ba.

## Jerin dubawa

- [ ] Ku duba bitar tsari ko ƙimar abin da ke aiki kafin ku dogara da bincike guda.
- [ ] Ku karanta bitar shaida da ke ƙarƙashi, ba kawai ƙimar taƙaitawa ko alama ba.
- [ ] Ku bayyana ingancin shaida da ƙarfin shawara a matsayin hukunce-hukunce biyu daban.
- [ ] Ku bambanta "babu shaidar tasiri" da "shaidar rashin tasiri" a fili a cikin taƙaitaccen bayanin da aka rubuta.
- [ ] Ku duba dacewar yanayi tsakanin binciken da aka duba da shawarar da ke hannu (jama'a, ma'auni, tsarin hidima).
- [ ] Ku tabbatar akwai tsarin kimantawar shaida cikin sauri kuma ana amfani da shi idan cikakkiyar bita ba za ta shiga jadawali ba.
- [ ] Ku amince da ƙofar shaida na shawarar kafin a duba sakamako.
- [ ] Ku shirya wallafa kimantawar wannan ƙungiya kanta, ko menene sakamakon.
- [ ] Ku kafa alaƙa ta dindindin da cibiyar abin da ke aiki ko ma'ajiyar share mai dacewa, ba gina ta daga tushe a ƙarƙashin matsi ba.
- [ ] Ku rubuta dalilin da ke bayan hukuncin ƙarshe, gami da shaidar da aka yi la'akari da ita da wadda aka ƙi.
- [ ] Ku duba shawarar bisa shawarar daraja da la'akari da halacci, ba ƙarfin shaida kaɗai ba.
